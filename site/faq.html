Privacy

Does my voice leave my Mac?
No. Every model runs locally on the Neural Engine. The only network traffic is the one-time download of a model you choose from the menu bar.
Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?
No. They stay on your Mac in ~/Pictures/Capipaste and on your clipboard until you paste them somewhere yourself.
Why does it need Screen Recording permission?
macOS requires it for any app that captures the screen. Capipaste only captures when you press ⌘⇧S and drag.

Pasting

Why does the terminal get a file path instead of the image?
Claude Code attaches an image from the clipboard and drops any text next to it. So when you capture from a terminal, Capipaste copies your note with [screenshot: /path.png], and the agent opens the file itself.
Which terminals count?
cmux, Ghostty, Terminal, iTerm2, Warp, WezTerm, kitty and Alacritty. Capipaste checks which app was in front when you pressed the shortcut.
What do chat apps get?
The annotated PNG and your note together. Codex pastes both. Some chat boxes keep only the image, and the saved file is always there if you need it.
Where does focus go after I press Return?
Back to the app you captured from, so ⌘V lands right away.

Speech

Which speech model should I pick?
Nemotron 3.5 Streaming if you want words to appear as you speak. Parakeet 110M for the smallest, fastest English model. Cohere Transcribe for the best accuracy on longer notes. Apple Speech works with no download at all.
Can I type instead of talk?
Yes. Click the note and type. Listening stops the moment you type, so dictation never overwrites your edits.
What if it mishears me?
Edit the text before pressing Return. If a transcript fails outright, Capipaste retries once, then keeps the card open and asks you to say it again.
Can I use a different microphone?
Pick any input from the Microphone list in the menu bar: the built-in mic, a USB mic or AirPods.

Capture & drawing

How do I capture a whole window?
After ⌘⇧S, press Space to switch the picker to window mode, then click the window.
How do I erase part of a line?
Press E and drag over it. The eraser removes only what's under its circle. ⌘Z undoes a whole drag.
Can I zoom in on small text?
Pinch on the trackpad or use ⌘+ and ⌘−. Two-finger scroll pans, ⌘0 resets. Anything you draw while zoomed lands in the right spot on the saved image.
Where do my screenshots go?
Into ~/Pictures/Capipaste, with your drawings flattened in at full resolution. Open the folder from the menu bar.

Setup

What do I need?
An Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 26. Building from source also needs Xcode and XcodeGen.
Can I change the shortcut?
It's ⌘⇧S today. A shortcut recorder is on the list.
Is it free?
Yes. Capipaste is MIT licensed and the source is on GitHub.
