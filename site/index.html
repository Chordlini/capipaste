01

Three keys between you and a clear bug report.

Step 1 · ⌘⇧S

Drag over what's wrong.

The native macOS picker opens. Drag a region, or press Space to grab a whole window. Esc backs out.

Step 2 · talk

Say what should change, circle where.

A card opens with your screenshot and starts listening. Draw on it while you talk. Your words appear as text, transcribed on your Mac.

Step 3 · ↩

Paste it where the work happens.

Return stops recording, copies the image and your note, and puts you back in the app you came from. ⌘V.

Small app. Every edge filed down.

Built for the loop you run fifty times a day: see it, say it, hand it off.

Speech stays on your Mac.

Transcription runs on the Neural Engine. Pick a model from the menu bar; downloads happen once, and nothing you say leaves the machine.

Knows where you're pasting.

From a terminal, Capipaste copies your note with the image path, so Claude Code reads the screenshot instead of swallowing your words. Everywhere else you get the image and the text.

Draw, erase, undo.

Circle the problem. The eraser removes only what's under it. ⌘Z steps back one stroke at a time.

Pinch into the pixels.

The card sizes itself to your capture. Pinch or ⌘+ to zoom, two fingers to pan, and marks land exactly where you drew them.

Asks again when it didn't catch that.

If a transcript fails, it retries. If that fails too, the card stays open and asks you to say it once more.

Any microphone, from the menu bar.

Switch between your built-in mic, a USB mic or AirPods without opening settings.

Every capture is kept.

Annotated PNGs are saved to ~/Pictures/Capipaste, and the note carries the path, so an agent can open the exact file later.

05

Questions.

Does my voice leave my Mac?
No. Every model runs locally. The only network traffic is the one-time download of a model you choose from the menu bar.
Why does the terminal get a file path instead of the image?
Claude Code attaches an image from the clipboard and drops any text next to it. So when you capture from a terminal, Capipaste copies your note with [screenshot: /path.png], and the agent opens the file itself.
Can I type instead of talk?
Yes. Click the note and type. Listening stops the moment you type, so dictation never overwrites your edits.
What if it mishears me?
Edit the text before pressing Return. If a transcript fails outright, Capipaste retries once, then keeps the card open and asks you to say it again.
Where do my screenshots go?
Into ~/Pictures/Capipaste, with your drawings flattened in at full resolution. Open the folder from the menu bar.
Which speech model should I pick?
Nemotron 3.5 Streaming if you want words to appear as you speak. Parakeet 110M if you want the smallest, fastest English model. Cohere Transcribe for the best accuracy on longer notes. Apple Speech works with no download at all.
